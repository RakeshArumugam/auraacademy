Who We Are & Our Purpose
A Space to Rediscover Your True Worth & Inner Strength
“Your circumstances may challenge you, but they need not determine your future. You don’t have to change who you are — you can begin by believing in who you can become.”
Every woman carries immense potential within her. Sometimes life challenges, societal expectations, grief, career transitions, or silent self-doubts make us lose sight of our personal power.

Founded by Subhasri S, a senior HR leader, Mind Health Counsellor, and Certified Life Coach, AURA Academy was born out of profound personal resilience. Having rebuilt her own life after personal heartbreak and life-altering turning points, Subhasri established AURA Academy to provide women with a compassionate sanctuary: a place where you will be heard without judgment, supported with empathy, and equipped with actionable tools to thrive.

Subhasri S is a senior Human Resources leader with over 25+ years of extensive experience in corporate people management, learning and organizational development. Having personally trained and mentored more than 1,000+ employees across multifaceted corporate environments, she brings an unmatched depth of wisdom to individual and group transformation.

A Journey of Unstoppable Resilience:
Subhasri’s life journey is a living testament to courage and perseverance. Having navigated formidable personal challenges from an early age, she learned that fortitude is forged in adversity. The unexpected loss of her husband in 2019 became a profound turning point. Rather than yielding to despair, she chose to rebuild her life, pursue advanced post-graduate qualifications in Counselling Psychology, and dedicate her existence to helping other women discover their self-worth.

Her life philosophy is beautifully distilled into four actionable words:
“Learn, Apply, Test and Teach from Experience.”
